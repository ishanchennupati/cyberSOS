# Phase 5 initial support, 2026-10-07

CyberSOS is independent. Jurisdiction: India. Entry labels are optional, changeable routing hints; every route uses the same conversation, canonical case and evidence review system. Labels do not promise universal crime coverage.

| Scenario | Initial capability | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suspected financial scam / unauthorized transaction | Distinguish citizen-authorized scam payment from unauthorized debit; known bank rails, amount, currency, references and timing; reviewed bank/1930 actions when supported | Bare loss/amount proves neither fraud nor a bank payment. Unknown timing is not urgency; wallet alone does not prove bank involvement. No recovery guarantee or liability calculation. |
| Online harassment, threats, private-image blackmail | Safe threat text, platform/profile/contact identifiers, preservation and supported platform reporting; 112 only for stated immediate physical danger | No intimate explicit images, CSAM, credentials or identity-document requests. Platform reporting is general flagging guidance, not a promise of removal. |
| Account takeover / impersonation | Current account-access facts, platform and profile identifiers, safe records; Google/Gmail official recovery/security handoff when current compromise is established | Specialized recovery guidance is initially Google/Gmail. Other providers are not declared verified recovery coverage. Impersonation alone proves neither account access nor bank involvement. |
| Mixed signals / mistaken entry hint | Preserve multiple supported signals; change hints, correct facts and reevaluate one shared plan | Separate incidents require clarification before their facts are combined. |

Readable JPG/JPEG, PNG and PDF, maximum 10 MB, use configured Gemini inline file understanding. Synthetic acceptance covers a readable screenshot, JPEG and single-page PDF financial record, non-explicit threat screenshot and account-security screenshot. Complex/scanned/multi-page document accuracy is not comprehensively evaluated. Timestamp text is retained without inventing a year/timezone. Quotes/pages, confidence and uncertainty belong to candidates; citizen review establishes canonical facts.

English is evaluated across the three initial routes. Roman Telugu and Hinglish are bounded regression journeys, not universal-language certification. Voice is Phase 9. Ransomware, cryptocurrency tracing, trafficking, all account providers and all crimes under the portal labels are outside initial proven coverage.

Private evidence remains incident-owned. Upload validation and private storage are reused. Evidence is hostile data, cannot create actions/tools or enter shared knowledge, and unreviewed candidates never become policy facts. Deleting an attachment removes its original and analysis/review derivations; previously confirmed canonical details remain with deleted-source provenance and honest conversation history. Replacement uses a new evidence identity. Full-case deletion/retention and expanded secure-return capabilities remain later-phase decisions; no launch retention promise is made here.

Reviewed sources: [NCRP FAQ](https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/FAQ.aspx), [MHA emergency response](https://www.mha.gov.in/en/commoncontent/emergency-response-support-system-erss), [Google compromised-account guidance](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6294825), [RBI unauthorized electronic banking circular](https://rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/Notification/PDFs/NOTI1506072017.PDF). Platform flagging, emergency and recovery handoffs are supported by these specific sources; CyberSOS does not claim that opening them submits anything. Native-file adapter references: [Gemini images](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-understanding), [Gemini PDFs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/document-processing), [pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing). No live browsing authority or paid fallback was added.
