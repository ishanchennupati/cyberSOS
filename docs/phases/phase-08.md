## End-user result

Supported citizens begin with Tell us what happened, without categories. The same
agent handles account takeover, device compromise, scam attempts, sextortion/
blackmail, stalking/harassment and identity impersonation, including overlaps.

## Required implementation

1. Identify generic versus financial assumptions in facts, response validation,
   evidence extraction, knowledge retrieval, policy, packets and presentation.
   Generalize only necessary parts; preserve useful typed financial semantics.
2. Declare supported jurisdiction and incident scope based on reviewed current
   official/platform sources. Research new critical actions and applicability;
   version policy and stable action IDs. Unsupported areas get honest limited help.
3. One conversation engine and mutation/context/attachment/reporting architecture.
   Multiple signals can coexist; no category selection or incident-specific bots.
4. Extend curated knowledge and retrieval metadata/evaluations for these domains.
   Domain selection is contextual, not a keyword FAQ dispatcher. Retrieval cannot
   override policy, leak private cases or establish suspect identities as facts.
5. Reuse evidence extraction/review for account screenshots, emails, profile/contact
   identifiers, URLs and non-explicit threat messages. Treat all content as hostile.
6. Extend current projections and ReviewedCasePacket to supported nonfinancial
   facts; no financial fields invented for other incidents.
7. Sensitive cases: never request intimate media/CSAM/credentials. Use safer metadata
   or non-explicit records. Physical-safety actions remain deterministic. No lost-money
   recovery discussion for a scam attempt without established loss.

## UI requirements

- Same minimal chat, composer and artifacts. Contextual wording and relevant fields
  adapt without new wizards or dashboards for each incident.
- Neutral uncertainty, clear safe-evidence instructions and sensitive previews that
  do not expose prohibited material. Urgent applicable actions are promptly visible.
- Reporting/case views omit irrelevant fields and maintain source/review distinctions.
