## Agreed conversation repairs — 2026-10-12

Distinguish an early urgent action from the fuller personalized outcome. An ACT NOW
card may appear before investigation finishes only when validated facts satisfy
the deterministic urgency policy; an entry hint alone never establishes urgency.
Useful missing questions may continue after that early help. Once the AI decides
there is sufficient useful understanding and presents the fuller plan and draft
case sheet, stop routine intake questions. Ask further questions only for a material
gap affecting safety, actions or reporting, an unresolved contradiction, or new
information; explain their purpose briefly. Keep natural conversation and updates
available without restarting intake or asking whether the citizen is done.

Repair repeated evidence-consent prompts. When the citizen says Yes to the active
offer to preserve/share safe supporting records, acknowledge that choice and show
the next practical attachment or typed-description affordance. Do not ask another
equivalent permission question. Consent does not establish that records exist,
were uploaded, were reviewed, or verify their contents. Preserve unknowns, allow
skip/cancel and keep uploads optional. Action completion must not replay a prior
question or restart an evidence-consent loop.

Verify the screenshot scenario through the Other Cyber Crime hint and equivalent
scenarios through the other hints: justified urgent help → useful investigation →
AI-decided fuller outcome → no routine questioning; records offer → natural Yes →
attachment/description affordance without another consent question. Also test
decline/skip, action completion, correction, new material information and reload.
These repairs are assigned to Phase 7; this document update does not implement them.
